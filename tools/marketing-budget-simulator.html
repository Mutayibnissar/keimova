Marketing Budget Simulator

Test how spend, cost per click, conversion rates and order economics change a campaign's estimated results. This is a planning calculator—not a forecast or promise of actual performance.

How to use it: Change assumptions to model a scenario. Use your own historical data where possible and test optimistic, base and conservative cases.

Average ad spend per click; must be above zero.

Modelled result

Read carefully: fractional conversions are mathematical expectations, not actual customers. Results omit taxes, refunds, shipping, payment fees, fixed overheads and attribution effects unless you include them in other costs. Gross margin is assumed constant.
