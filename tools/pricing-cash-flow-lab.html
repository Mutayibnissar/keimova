How to interpret this model

  • Break-even is a contribution-based estimate; it is unavailable when contribution per unit is zero or negative.
  • Operating profit uses variable cost for units sold; the cash-flow bridge uses the supplier cash payment you enter. These are intentionally separate.
  • Closing cash is a simplified estimate and may be negative. Runway is shown only when monthly net cash flow is negative and closing cash is positive.
  • This tool excludes taxes, financing, depreciation, inventory changes beyond the entered cash payment, seasonality, returns, mixed products and uncertain demand. It is for learning, not financial advice or a forecast.
