# Keimova course-source map and review backlog

**Reviewed:** 15 October 2026  
**Purpose:** Replace unsupported generic course-writing with a source-first workflow. This is a source map and audit plan, not a claim that every current lesson has already passed a line-by-line scholarly review.

## Curriculum decision

Keimova should use established, openly accessible courses/textbooks as the curricular spine wherever suitable. Build an original study interface, explanations, worked examples, and practice activities around those sources. Do not present AI-generated prose as evidence. Google Scholar is for finding research; each selected paper must be checked at its publisher, DOI landing page, or institutional repository.

**Reuse rule:** Prefer linking to complete original courses/textbooks and writing original explanations. Do not copy chapters, question banks, figures, or assessments without checking the exact licence and permission. An open-access webpage is not automatically openly licensed. Confirm commercial reuse terms before incorporating any material into a commercial product.

## Required lesson-level evidence record

Before a lesson is marked reviewed, add a record containing:
1. Lesson title and version.
2. Claim or learning objective.
3. Source title, author/organisation, date, source type, DOI/canonical URL.
4. Exact chapter/section/page supporting the claim.
5. Access/review date.
6. Evidence strength and limitation (for research: design, sample/context, association vs causation).
7. Licence/reuse status.
8. Reviewer and unresolved questions.

## Publication gate

Do not label a course "verified", "research-backed", or "peer-reviewed" solely because it links to Google Scholar or a reputable institution. Until lesson-level checks and answer-key checks are completed, label it **source-mapped — review in progress**. If a claim has no suitable source, remove it, soften it, or mark it as an illustrative hypothesis. Use official existing course pages as the learning reference when they offer better coverage than a short custom summary.
