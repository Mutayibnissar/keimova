## Knowledge-check review

- Q1 key: **b** — define the problem, objective and evidence needs before choosing a solution. The distractors describe premature or unfocused action.
- Q2 key: **c** — the intended definition includes groups that affect or are affected by an organisation.
- Q3 key: **a** — the question tests analysis before response rather than automatic price matching or ignoring competition.
- The answer keys and explanations in the HTML were checked for internal consistency with the stated learning objectives. This is a content logic check, not external validation or psychometric testing.
- The quiz has three items only; it should not be described as a comprehensive or validated assessment.
